This repository is Dispatch, an incident management platform. Someone who comes late to an incident's channel can get a machine-written briefing on what they missed. When several latecomers ask one after another, Dispatch does not always write a fresh briefing: it may hand out an earlier one again. Which function decides between handing out an earlier briefing and writing a new one, how old may a briefing be and still be handed out again, and which function finds that earlier briefing? Do not change any code.

Finish your answer with these four lines, in this exact form:
FILE: <path of the file that defines the deciding function>
FUNCTION: <name of the deciding function>
MAX_AGE: <how old a briefing may be and still be handed out again, number and unit>
LOOKUP: <name of the function that finds the earlier briefing>
